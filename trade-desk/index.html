How this works
  1. Each trading evening and again before the open, Market Radar searches real business news (company announcements, results, orders, approvals, policy, global cues) and writes a plan for the next session: at most 6 picks, each with an entry window, entry price zone, target, stop, and a time to exit if neither is hit.
  2. This page checks every pick against live NSE prices every 30 seconds: waiting, watching for entry, in trade (with live profit/loss), target hit, stopped out, or time exit.
  3. After the close, each pick is scored with strict rules: no fill unless price entered the zone inside the window, and if the stop and target are touched in the same 5 minutes, it counts as a loss.
Automated research from public news, not investment advice and not SEBI-registered research. News-based intraday calls fail often; judge this system by its track record above, start with small amounts, and always use your own stop-loss. Prices from Yahoo Finance may be delayed. Brokerage, taxes and slippage are not included in returns.
